Privacy Policy for LexPad: File & Code Editor

Last updated: November 12, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how your information is handled in LexPad: File & Code Editor (package com.johnan.lexpad, referred to as the "App"), developed by Nanzen.

We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it. Our architectural philosophy is built on client-side privacy, local data ownership, and zero server storage of user files or document content.

Information Collection and Use

The App does not collect, transmit, or store any personal documents, source code, text files, or personally identifiable information (PII) on Nanzen servers. No user account or cloud registration is required to use the App, and there is no advertising tracking or personal profile building.

100% Local Document & Scratchpad Storage

All files, URLs, JSON payloads, and scratchpad buffers are processed and stored strictly locally on your device:

  • Scratchpad Buffers & Drafts: Scratchpad buffers, unsaved drafts, and document metadata are stored solely within the App's private internal storage (filesDir/buffers/). These files remain entirely on your device and are never uploaded to any server.
  • Storage Access Framework (SAF): When opening, editing, or saving external files via Android's native file picker (OpenDocument and CreateDocument), the App reads and writes content directly at the URI location chosen by you. The App does not copy external files to remote servers or retain unauthorized background access.
  • Clipboard & Developer Tools: All clipboard operations, percent-encoding/decoding, URL query parameter splitting/joining, and JSON formatting/minifying occur strictly in-memory within the local device process.

Biometric App Lock & Security

You may optionally enable biometric authentication (fingerprint or face unlock) or device credentials to guard access to the App:

  • Hardware Keystore & BiometricPrompt: The App uses the standard AndroidX BiometricPrompt API provided by the Android operating system.
  • Secure Enclave Protection: Biometric authentication is performed locally by the Android OS hardware keystore and secure enclave. Biometric templates, biometric sensor readings, and credentials never leave your device's hardware security module and are never accessible to the App or Nanzen.

Third-Party Service Providers

To deliver in-app updates, optional Pro license upgrades, and ensure application stability, the App utilizes select third-party services. These services may collect technical, non-personally identifiable device information and crash diagnostics:

  • Google Play Services & Google Play Billing: Facilitates seamless Google Play In-App Updates and manages optional Pro upgrades (such as lifetime unlock pro_version_unlock and monthly subscription pro_subscription_monthly). Google processes billing transactions according to Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Firebase Crashlytics: Collects anonymized crash diagnostics, stack traces, device hardware models, and operating system versions to help us fix stability bugs and crashes.
  • Firebase Analytics: Collects aggregated, non-personally identifiable app usage statistics and session metrics to help us prioritize features and performance optimizations.

Cookies and Tracking Identifiers

The App does not use cookies. Google Play Services and Firebase SDKs may use pseudonymous device identifiers or app instance IDs strictly to facilitate update distribution, license verification, and aggregated diagnostics. You can manage or reset app identifiers in your Android device settings.

Data Security

We value your trust and implement security best practices. All file reading and writing operations utilize Android's secure sandboxed app storage and native Storage Access Framework permissions. No document contents, URLs, or scratchpad buffers ever route through intermediate Nanzen proxy servers or cloud sync infrastructure.

Links to External Sites

The App or its documentation may contain links to external websites (such as open-source repositories, documentation, or developer resources). If you tap an external link, you will be directed to that third-party site. These external sites are not operated by us, and we encourage you to review their respective privacy policies. We assume no responsibility for the content or practices of any third-party websites.

Children's Privacy

The App is a developer utility and text editor and does not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13. If you become aware that a child has provided personal information to third-party services via the App, please contact us so that we can take appropriate measures.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect application improvements or regulatory requirements. Any updates will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.
